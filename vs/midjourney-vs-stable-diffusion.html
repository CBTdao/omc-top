The verdict

If you want beautiful images today with zero fiddling, Midjourney is worth every dollar of the $10 plan. If you want ownership, custom styles and no monthly fee — and you're willing to learn ComfyUI — Stable Diffusion wins. No GPU to run it on? That's exactly the gap decentralized compute networks like OMC are filling.

Straight answers

Can I run Stable Diffusion without my own GPU?

Yes — rent by the hour from cloud GPU providers, or use hosting services with a monthly fee. Decentralized compute networks (like OMC, this site's sponsor-less banner above) are also emerging for exactly this.

Is Midjourney worth $10/month?

If images are part of your output — yes, easily. It replaces a stock-photo subscription and a designer's first draft in the same click.

Who owns the images I generate?

SD self-hosted: everything is yours. Midjourney: paid plans get broad commercial rights, but read the current terms — they've changed before.
