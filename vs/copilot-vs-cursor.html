The verdict

If your team lives across many IDEs and you want the lowest-cost AI tier, Copilot is the pragmatic pick. If you're willing to move your whole workflow into an AI-native editor, Cursor is the stronger daily driver in 2026 — the agent mode alone saves hours a week. Many developers run both: Copilot in JetBrains for work, Cursor for side projects.

Straight answers

Can I use both at once?

Yes, and most working developers do: Copilot inside JetBrains or Neovim for the day job, Cursor for the greenfield project that's eating your nights.

Is Cursor just VS Code with a theme?

It's a fork, but the AI plumbing goes deep — project-wide context, agent edits across files, and a codebase you can literally ask questions of. Try the agent mode once and the fork detail stops mattering.

Will AI coding tools replace junior developers?

They replace typing, not judgment. Every tool here still needs someone who knows what 'correct' looks like — that someone is currently being paid more, not less.
