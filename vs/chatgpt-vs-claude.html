What the landing pages won't tell you

ChatGPT

The $20 plan increasingly feels like the trailer for the $200 plan. Usage limits arrive exactly when you're on a roll.

Claude

The best writer in the business — and the strictest bouncer. Hit your limit at 2pm on a Tuesday and you're done for the day.

Gemini

The free tier is genuinely excellent — and the fine print is your data, plus the occasional answer delivered with total confidence and zero accuracy.

The verdict

Pick ChatGPT for the best all-round assistant with the richest ecosystem. Pick Claude if your work is code, long documents or writing quality — it is the writer's and developer's pick. Pick Gemini if you want the most capable free option or already live in Google's ecosystem. Many power users quietly pay for two of them.

Straight answers

Which one is the smartest?

Wrong question. All three are smarter than most tasks require. Pick by workflow: where your files live, what you make, and which app you're willing to keep open.

Can I just use all three free tiers?

Yes — and you should, for at least a week, before paying anyone. Most people discover their 'must-have' plan was a habit, not a need.

Which handles Chinese best?

ChatGPT leads for nuance, Claude for long Chinese documents, Gemini is fine. If Chinese is your main language, also look at DeepSeek — it's free and shockingly good.
