Ylopo8.7

AI-driven lead nurture on top of your IDX: behavior-triggered texts, retargeting ads and an assistant that follows up like a persistent human.

Pricing: From ~$500/mo per marketBest for: Teams buying digital leads at volume

Structurely8.4

The AI inside sales agent: instant text/email responses, qualification questions and appointment booking, 24/7.

Pricing: From ~$200-500/moBest for: Solo agents drowning in portal leads

HouseCanary8.1

Valuation and risk analytics with genuine ML depth — the CMA your listing appointment can defend.

Pricing: Per-report / enterprise tiersBest for: Listing presentations, investor clients

kvCORE (BoldTrail AI)7.9

The all-in-one platform most brokerages already pay for; BoldTrail adds AI nudges to a CRM everyone half-uses.

Pricing: Bundled via brokerage / ~$500/moBest for: Agents inside an existing kvCORE shop
5

Revaluate7.6

Predicts which contacts in your database are about to move — a smaller promise, aimed at the database you already own.

Pricing: From ~$60/mo (db size tiers)Best for: Agents with 2,000+ stale contacts

The verdict

Source the money before the tool: if your problem is lead response speed, Structurely is the highest-ROI line item on this page; if it's lead quality, Ylopo's nurture layer is worth the price. HouseCanary earns its keep in listing appointments, not in software adoption. And if your brokerage already provides kvCORE, start by actually using it — the most expensive AI tool is the one you pay for and ignore.
