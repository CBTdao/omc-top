Google Veo 3.19.2

The quality king: native audio, cinematic consistency, and the physics rarely lie. Access through Gemini or Flow.

Pricing: Included with Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo) · API ~$0.10–0.75/secBest for: Brand films, ads, anything with dialogue
2

Runway Gen-4 / 4.58.9

The professional's studio: Motion Brush, camera controls, Act-Two performance capture — shot-level control nobody matches.

Pricing: Standard $12–15/mo (625 credits) · Pro $28–35 (2,250)Best for: Agencies, filmmakers, precise direction
3

Kling 3.08.5

Best cents-per-usable-second in the category, with image-to-video output that survives a feed scroll.

Pricing: From ~$6.99/mo (660 credits) · Pro ~$25.99 (3,000)Best for: High-volume social and UGC-style ads
4

Pika 2.x8.0

The friendliest interface in the group: PikaFrames keyframes, one-tap effects, and a genuinely usable free tier.

Pricing: Free tier · from ~$10/moBest for: Beginners, social content, experiments
5

Sora 2 (via ChatGPT)7.8

Still a quality benchmark — but the standalone app shut down in April and the API sunset in September. Do not build on it.

Pricing: Included with ChatGPT Plus ($20) / ProBest for: Existing ChatGPT subscribers only

The verdict

Route by job: Kling or Runway image-to-video for anything that must survive a client review, Veo 3.1 for cinematic hero shots with dialogue, Pika to learn on. Budget for a real rejection rate — every model here nails one take and warps the next nineteen. And don't build a business on Sora right now: OpenAI sunset the standalone app in April and the API in September.
