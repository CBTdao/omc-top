QuickBooks (Intuit Assist)8.8

The default ledger with AI bolted in properly: anomaly flags, cash-flow forecasting and invoice drafting inside the tool your clients already use.

Pricing: From ~$35/mo + AI tiersBest for: Firms serving small business clients
2

Xero (JAX)8.5

JAX reads bank feeds and proposes the coding workbooks used to bill hours for. Cleanest API story for building your own automations.

Pricing: From ~$20/mo + JAX tiersBest for: Tech-forward practices, APAC/UK
3

Vic.ai8.2

Autonomous invoice processing for high-volume AP: extraction, coding, approval routing with a real audit trail.

Pricing: Enterprise quote (volume-based)Best for: AP teams processing 10k+ invoices/mo
4

FloQast8.0

Month-end close checklists, reconciliation matching and flux analysis — AI where the work actually hurts.

Pricing: From ~$300/mo (quote)Best for: Controllers running the close in-house
5

Karbon7.8

Practice management with AI drafting: it doesn't do the bookkeeping, it stops work from falling through cracks at scale.

Pricing: From ~$49/user/moBest for: Firms with 10+ staff, messy handoffs

The verdict

If your clients are on QuickBooks, Intuit Assist is the least disruptive upgrade you can make — adoption beats sophistication when your clients are the ones uploading receipts. Xero's JAX wins on API openness if you build your own pipeline. Vic.ai and FloQast are enterprise-grade answers to volume problems a small firm doesn't have yet; buying them early means paying enterprise prices to automate work you can still do manually. Karbon is the sleeper pick: the practice-management lift is often bigger than the bookkeeping automation everyone demos at conferences.
