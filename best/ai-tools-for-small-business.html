Meeting jargon? AITop's AI glossary explains it in plain English.

1

ChatGPT (Plus)9.0

The general layer every business should start with: writing, research, planning, customer communication.

Pricing: Free tier · Plus $20/mo · Team ~$25–30/userBest for: The first AI subscription you buy
2

Zapier (AI Actions)8.6

The glue between your apps: forms to CRM to follow-ups, with AI models wired into the middle of workflows.

Pricing: Free (100 tasks/mo) · Professional ~$19.99/moBest for: Killing repetitive admin work
3

Notion (Business)8.2

Your operating manual that answers questions itself: Notion Agent, AI meeting notes, enterprise search.

Pricing: Business ~$20/member/mo (Agent included) · AI add-on ~$10Best for: Teams drowning in internal Q&A
4

QuickBooks (Simple Start)8.0

AI bookkeeping that categorizes expenses and flags anomalies — with a mandatory human review on anything that touches the books.

Pricing: Simple Start ~$38/mo before promosBest for: Finance hygiene, not finance decisions
5

HubSpot CRM7.8

A genuinely free CRM with AI email drafting, call summaries and lead scoring — enough for year one.

Pricing: Free CRM · paid plans from ~$20/seatBest for: Anyone still tracking leads in a spreadsheet

The verdict

Buy three, not ten: ChatGPT ($20) + Zapier ($20) + Notion (~$20/member) covers content, admin and knowledge for about $60 a month — most of it replaceable by free tiers while you validate. Run any workflow manually for two weeks before automating it, measure time saved for 30 days, and never let AI touch the books without a human review step. A wrong blog draft is annoying; a misclassified expense at scale is a different problem.
