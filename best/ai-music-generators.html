Best AI music generators of 2026

Data last updated: 2026-10-05. Pricing may have changed — verify with the vendor.

The 30-second answer

The one question that decides this category is not sound quality — it is whether you can get the file out and legally publish it. Suno V6 makes the best full songs and, on Pro or Premier, grants commercial rights on what you download; the catch is a hard monthly download cap (20 on Pro, 60 on Premier). ElevenLabs Music is the producer's pick if you already use the platform for voice and want stems and MP3/WAV exports. Mureka is the value play with voice cloning and 12-stem splitting on Premier. And Udio — still rated highly for fidelity on most lists — has disabled all audio, video and stem downloads since 29 October 2025, so a finished Udio track cannot leave the app today. Treat every "best AI music tool" comparison that doesn't mention that as out of date.

What the landing pages won't tell you

The vendor

"Generate unlimited songs" is not the same as "keep unlimited songs." Suno Pro lets you make roughly 500 tracks a month but allows only 20 downloads; extra downloads are sold separately. Always read the download row, not the credits row — that is what you actually get to keep.

The listicles

Half of the "best AI music generators" articles rank Udio in the top two on sound quality alone, without noting that downloads have been off since 29 October 2025. Its songs cannot be exported, so they cannot be released, synced or monetized. A tool you cannot extract a file from is not a production tool.

The licensing

A commercial license is not copyright ownership. The US Copyright Office has held that purely AI-generated output generally cannot be copyrighted, and Suno and Udio both face active litigation (Sony and Universal filed over V6 on 18 September 2026). Keep your subscription records for the tracks you release, and prefer plans that state commercial rights in writing.

The verdict

Choose by what has to leave the app, not by which demo sounded best. If you want finished songs with vocals and intend to release them, Suno on Pro is the default — budget for the 20-downloads-a-month cap, because that, not generation, is the bottleneck. If you produce and want stems, MIDI or an API in a pipeline you already own, ElevenLabs Music is the better fit and shares credits with its voice tools. If voice cloning or 12-stem control matters and cost per released track does, Mureka undercuts Suno. If you only need instrumental beds you will keep editing in a DAW, Soundverse gives you MIDI and stems. And if fidelity is all you care about, understand that Udio is now an in-app experience: you can listen, you cannot publish.

Straight answers

Can I download songs from Udio?

No — not as of 29 October 2025. Udio disabled audio, video and stem downloads for all users, including paid tiers, as part of its licensing transition with Universal Music Group. You can still generate songs and share a udio.com link, but there is no way to export a file to release, monetize or distribute.

Does Suno let me use generated music commercially?

Yes, on paid plans. Suno's terms state that songs you download as a paying subscriber carry commercial rights. The free plan does not include commercial rights or stem separation, and downloads are capped even on paid tiers (about 20 a month on Pro, 60 on Premier), with extras sold separately.

Which AI music generator lets me release to Spotify?

Only some plans do. Suno grants commercial rights on paid downloads; ElevenLabs requires Creator or above for streaming distribution (Starter is commercial but excludes streaming, film, TV and studio games); Mureka paid plans include full commercial rights. Beatoven and Mubert do not allow streaming distribution on any plan, and Udio cannot export at all.

Is AI-generated music copyrightable?

Generally no, if it is purely AI-generated — the US Copyright Office has held that output lacking human authorship cannot be protected. A commercial license from the platform lets you use and monetize the track, but it does not give you copyright ownership. Keep records of the subscription and plan active when each track was generated.
