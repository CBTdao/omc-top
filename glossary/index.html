The 30-second answer

AI tool marketing runs on vocabulary you were never taught. This glossary covers the terms that actually change purchase decisions — what a token costs, how much context a model really uses, whether "unlimited" means you can keep the file, and who owns what you generate. Every definition is one paragraph, every entry ends with a checklist, and every claim is dated 2026.

Pricing & paying

  • AI token — the metered unit every API bill and "unlimited" plan is quietly measured in. What one costs, and why reasoning models triple the meter.
  • Commercial-use rights (AI music) — the difference between making a song and being allowed to release it. Download caps, streaming tiers, and the Udio export freeze.

Models & accuracy

  • Context window — how much a model can hold in mind at once, and why the advertised number and the usable number are different things.
  • RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) — how AI search tools look things up before answering, and why citation quality is the tell of a good one.
  • AI hallucination — when models invent facts with total confidence, which tools fight it hardest, and the checks that catch it.

Agents & automation

  • AI agent — a model given tools and a loop, so it acts instead of only answering. In 2026 the word also became a billing unit: what counts as "resolved" now sets your invoice.

Audio & media

  • Voice cloning — recreating a voice from samples: what it takes, where the consent rules now bite, and which platforms let you do it legally.
  • Stem separation — splitting a finished track back into vocals, drums and bass. The producer's feature that separates toys from tools.
  • AI watermarking & provenance — invisible labels on AI-generated media, C2PA credentials, and what platforms now demand before they host your output.

How we write these

Every entry answers three questions in order: what the term actually means, why it should change which tool you buy, and what is true in 2026 specifically — with the dates and numbers we'd want if we were the ones spending. When a claim is contested or a vendor behaves badly, we say so on the record. Our rankings and comparisons follow the same rules; the editorial policy has the details, including how OMC's involvement is disclosed and walled off.

New here? Start from the rankings — best AI chatbots, best AI music generators, Perplexity vs Google AI Mode vs ChatGPT Search — and dip back into a definition whenever a landing page starts talking about tokens and context like you already know.

The bottom line

Jargon is not neutral — it is written to sell. A vendor that quotes context windows without effective-use data, or "unlimited" plans without download rows, is counting on the vocabulary gap. Learn the nine terms above and most AI tool landing pages lose their power to impress and regain their power to inform. That is the whole point of a glossary: it is not homework, it is armor.
