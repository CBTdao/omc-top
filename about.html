About & editorial policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What AITop is

AITop is an independent comparison site for AI tools and business software. Most AI reviews are several thousand words of marketing restated as opinion. We publish the opposite: one table, real prices, real weaknesses, and a verdict you can act on. Every page states the date its data was last verified.

We cover chat assistants, image generation, coding tools, and — because that is where readers lose the most money — professional software for legal, insurance, accounting and real-estate work.

Who runs it

AITop is operated by the team behind the Omniverse Compute (OMC) ecosystem, a decentralized GPU compute network on BNB Chain. We disclose this because it is relevant to how you read our coverage:

  • OMC runs compute infrastructure. We review AI tools, not compute networks, and we do not rank or score OMC or any competing compute network on this Site.
  • Our coverage is funded by advertising (Google AdSense) sold by Google. It is not funded by vendors, and vendors cannot buy a position in a ranking.
  • We publish no affiliate links. If that ever changes, the relevant page will say so in plain language at the top.
  • Team members are pseudonymous by project policy. Editorial decisions are attributed to the editorial team as a whole rather than to individuals.

How we test

  1. We pay for the plans ourselves. No sponsored accounts, no vendor-supplied demos used as the basis of a review.
  2. At least two weeks of real work per tool. We use each product on actual deadlines rather than curated demo prompts.
  3. We test the free tier too. Where a tool has a usable free plan, we say so — "best" does not always mean "most expensive".
  4. We re-check prices monthly. Each page displays the date it was last verified. If a vendor changes something and we miss it, that date tells you how stale the page might be.
  5. We name the weaknesses. Every ranking entry carries a "what the landing page won't tell you" note. No tool is presented as flawless.

How we rank

Rankings weigh four things, in roughly this order: whether the tool does the job it claims; total cost at a realistic usage level; how much friction it adds to a normal workflow; and how the vendor behaves on data handling, exportability and lock-in. Scores are our own editorial judgement, not an aggregate of third-party benchmarks — where we cite outside data, we name the source and date.

Corrections policy

We get things wrong sometimes: a vendor changes a price, renames a plan, or ships a feature we did not see. If you spot an error, email us with the page URL and what is incorrect.

  • Factual errors (wrong price, wrong feature, wrong plan name) are corrected as soon as we verify them.
  • Substantive corrections are noted on the page itself, together with the revised verification date.
  • We do not remove or downgrade a review because a vendor is unhappy with it. Requests to alter editorial conclusions are declined.

Advertising and independence

The Site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Ads are selected by Google, not by us, and advertisers have no visibility into our drafts and no influence over our rankings. We do not accept payment for coverage, placement, or a better score, and we do not run paid "sponsored" listicles.

Not professional advice

Pages covering legal, insurance, accounting or real-estate software are general information, not professional advice. Suitability depends on your jurisdiction, licensing rules and firm policies. Confirm current terms, compliance certifications and pricing with the vendor, and consult a qualified adviser where it matters. See our Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.
